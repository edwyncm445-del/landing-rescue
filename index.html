Asked before every rescue

Fair

Questions

Which website platforms do you support?

Almost all of them. We can hand over a self-contained page for any host, or implement directly on common platforms — WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and similar — wherever you can grant us access. We'll confirm the exact approach for your setup during the free review, before you pay anything.

Can you redesign only part of my website?

Yes — that's exactly what Hero Rescue (£99) is for. If your page mostly works but the top section lets it down, we'll rebuild that part properly: headline, supporting copy, call-to-action and a layout that behaves on every screen size.

Is hosting included?

No — and that's deliberate. We don't resell hosting or lock you in. Your redesigned page runs on the hosting and domain you already have, and if you ever leave us, everything stays working and yours.

How long does a project take?

We agree a delivery date with you individually before any payment, based on the package, the current queue and the state of your page. What we won't do is quote a fake "48 hours!" number to win the job, then miss it.

What happens after I pay?

Your rescue enters the build queue on the agreed date. We share progress as we go, send you the first draft for your revision round(s), make the changes, then hand over — either as files you can keep or installed directly on your platform.

Are revisions included?

Yes — one revision round is included with Hero Rescue and Full Landing Page, and two with Conversion Rescue. A revision is a considered set of changes to the draft; if you later want something outside the original scope, we'll quote it honestly before doing the work.

Can you guarantee better conversion rates?

No — and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. Conversion depends on your offer, your prices, your traffic and your reputation, not just the page. What we can honestly promise: a clearer, faster-to-understand, professionally built page that stops wasting the visitors you already have.
