This is a working template for the Landing Rescue terms of service. It reflects our intended way of working but has not been reviewed by a lawyer or a qualified adult. Do not rely on it until the draft banner is removed.

1. What we sell

We sell fixed-scope website redesign services ("the Packages") at the prices published on our homepage. Each Package lists exactly what is included. Anything not listed is not included unless we agree it in writing.

2. How a project starts

  • You send an enquiry with your website address and requirements.
  • We review your site and confirm scope, price and a delivery date in writing.
  • If you accept, we send a secure payment link (processed by Stripe).
  • Work begins once payment is received. The agreed delivery date is part of the written scope, not a generic promise on this website.

3. Revisions

Hero Rescue and Full Landing Page include one revision round; Conversion Rescue includes two. A revision round is a single consolidated set of requested changes to the draft we deliver. New requirements added after scope is agreed may be quoted separately, and we will always tell you before doing billable work.

4. What we don't promise

We design and build to a professional standard, but we do not guarantee any specific commercial outcome — including increased sales, enquiries or conversion rates — and delivery dates are those individually agreed per project. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

5. Your content and access

You confirm you own or have permission to use the text, images and branding you ask us to work with, and you will provide reasonable access to your website or platform when implementation is included. We keep access credentials confidential and remove them at handover.

6. Hosting and handover

Hosting is not included. On completion we hand over working files or implement the redesign on your existing hosting or platform, as agreed in the scope. You own the delivered work on receipt of full payment.

7. Cancellations and refunds

You may cancel before work begins for a full refund. Once work has started, we refund any part of the fee that fairly corresponds to work not yet carried out. If we fail to deliver the agreed scope, you are entitled to a proportionate remedy.

8. The law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. The final reviewed version of this document will name the legal entity and a correspondence address; until then, contact landingrescue.business@gmail.com.
