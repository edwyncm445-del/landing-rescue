The method

Nothing rushed.
Nothing skipped.

01 /

Inspect & wash

Your car is photographed and inspected under LED lighting, then washed properly — two buckets, pH-neutral foam, no swirl-making shortcuts.

02 /

Decontaminate

Iron fallout, tar and bonded contaminants are dissolved and clayed away, so what remains is honest paint — ready to be measured.

03 /

Correct

Paint depth is measured panel by panel before machine polishing removes the defects that washing never could.

04 /

Protect & cure

Sealant or ceramic coating is applied in controlled conditions and left to cure. You collect a car that beads water like it's annoyed by it.
