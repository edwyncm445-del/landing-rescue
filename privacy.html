Privacy notice

Draft — requires review by a qualified adult before this site goes live

This is a working template for the Landing Rescue privacy notice. It has been written to reflect how the site is actually built — but it has not been reviewed by a lawyer or a qualified adult, and it must not be relied upon until it has been.

1. Who we are

Landing Rescue (“we”, “us”) is a small UK web design business. You can contact us at landingrescue.business@gmail.com. Once the business is formally registered, the legal entity name, address and registration details should be inserted here.

2. What this site collects

  • Enquiry form: if you send us an enquiry, we receive your name, email address, business name, website URL, chosen package and your message. We use this only to respond to you and to scope and deliver any work you agree to.
  • Emails: if you email us directly, we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Analytics: this template site does not set tracking cookies. If analytics are added later (e.g. privacy-friendly analytics), this section must be updated before they are switched on.
  • Fonts: this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and discloses your IP address to Google as part of receiving the font files.

3. What we never do

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or add you to a marketing list without your explicit consent. Stripe payment links are handled entirely by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.

4. How long we keep things

Enquiry emails are kept while the conversation is active and for up to 12 months afterwards, then deleted unless you become a customer, in which case records are kept as required for tax and accounting law.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email landingrescue.business@gmail.com and we will respond within one calendar month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

6. Changes

When this notice is finalised, the "draft" banner will be removed and the date below will show when it took effect. Until then, treat this page as a placeholder.
