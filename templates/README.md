# Landing Rescue — future sector template system

There are now two independent working design studies:

- `work/london-roofing/index.html` — trades and roofing, compact single-file layout.
- `work/garden-maintenance-scotland/index.html` — lifestyle-led landscaping and garden maintenance, modular `lifestyle.css` and `lifestyle.js`, with photography-led services, gallery filters, lightbox, area shortcuts and an honest demonstration form.

Both are **unofficial design concepts**, not approved client projects or a client's original work.

Use the second project's separation of HTML/CSS/JS as the starting structure for future sector templates. Use a distinctive visual language for each sector rather than simply recolouring the exact same layout.

## Reuse this design system, not the client's identity

For each new sector example:
1. Research a real business, or create a clearly fictional brand.
2. Define the visitor's problem, audience, trust signals, services and primary enquiry action.
3. Duplicate the page into a new `work/<sector-project>/index.html` folder.
4. Replace the business name, service descriptions, service-area list, contact details, FAQs and images.
5. Use a distinctive design direction appropriate for the industry instead of simply changing text and colours.
6. Never invent client testimonials, certifications, years in business, projects, results, awards or approved partnerships.
7. Keep speculative work clearly marked as an independent concept.
8. Keep contact forms as honest demonstrations until the client approves the concept and provides a working, secure form endpoint.
9. Check keyboard navigation, phone layout, screen-reader labels, broken links, actual image permissions and HTML/JS validity.
10. Add a new preview card to the main Landing Rescue `index.html` only **after** the complete live example is tested.

## Useful sector starting points

- Trades and home services: immediate quote route, service categories, locations and proof of workmanship.
- Hospitality: menu or offering, opening hours, directions and reservations.
- Automotive: service comparison, gallery and quote/booking route.
- Professional services: trust, expertise, service explanation and consultation route.

## Important

- Do not upload personal customer information into demo forms.
- Treat external stock photos as illustrative unless the business provides licensed job photos.
- The roofing example intentionally **does not send** form submissions. It validates inputs and shows an accurate preview confirmation.
- Keep the public portfolio small: show only your strongest functioning examples.

## Before / after portfolio presentations

- Use a real, functioning original-site link for the *before* item.
- Use the actual interactive concept URL for the *after* item.
- Do not fabricate original screenshots or conversion results.
- If you later capture genuine screenshots, label the date of capture and website URL for each.
- Identify stock photography and separate it from client project imagery.

## Sector-design contrast (November 2026)

The garden concept has been rebuilt as a **light editorial lifestyle site**: centre-set typography, a full-width photo hero, cream/sage palette, rounded service cards, a stock-photo inspiration gallery and a softer quote journey. It is purposefully visually distinct from the charcoal/terracotta roofing concept.

Reusable pattern: copy the *technical structure* (accessible mobile nav, relative CSS/JS, gallery controls, validated demo form) but choose new layout, typography, palette and visual hierarchy for each industry. The separate files are `index.html`, `lifestyle.css` and `lifestyle.js`.
